# LinkedIn Connection Request Templates

> Max 300 characters. Personalize the [bracketed] fields before sending.
> Tip: Add 1–2 specific lines showing you've noticed something real about their work.

---

## Template A — EHS / Safety Head

```
Hi [Name], I work with [Automotive/FMCG/Pharma] plants in TN & Karnataka on
machine safety — reducing man-machine interface risks and building stronger safety
systems. Your role at [Company] caught my attention. Would love to connect.
— [Your name]
```

---

## Template B — Plant Head / Factory Manager

```
Hi [Name], I partner with plant heads at large manufacturing facilities in South
India on machine safety — audits, guarding, and safety system integration. Have
worked with [similar company type] plants. Would love to connect and exchange notes.
```

## Template C — CXO / Operations Head

```
Hi [Name], I work with manufacturing leaders in TN & Karnataka on building robust

```

## Template D — After viewing their post/comment (personalized)

```
Hi [Name], your recent post on [safety topic / near-miss / ISO journey] resonated
with me. I work with manufacturing plants in TN & Karnataka on exactly this —
[machine guarding / safety audits / ISO 45001 implementation]. Would love to connect.
```

---

## Personalization Notes

- **For ISO-certified companies**: mention "supporting ISO 45001 journey" or "building on your safety culture"
- **For automotive plants**: reference "press safety, robotic cell guarding, conveyor interlocks"
- **For FMCG/packaging**: reference "high-speed machinery, LOTO programs, conveyor guards"
- **For pharma**: reference "ATEX zones, chemical handling, confined space safety"
- **For metals**: reference "molten metal, overhead crane safety, hot work permits"

## InMail Subject Lines (for 2nd/3rd degree leads)

2. `Safety audit results from [similar company in their segment]`
3. `One question — [Company]'s ISO 45001 journey`
4. `15 min? — safety risk I noticed for [segment] plants`
5. `[Company] + machine safety — worth a quick chat?`
