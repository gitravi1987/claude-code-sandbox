# InMail Templates

> Use for 2nd/3rd degree leads who haven't accepted a connection request.
> Keep it under 300 words. Lead with something specific to them, not about you.

---

## InMail A — ISO 45001 / Safety Culture Signal

**Subject**: `Quick question on [Company]'s machine safety approach`

```
Hi [Name],

I came across [Company]'s commitment to [ISO 45001 / Zero Harm / safety excellence]
and it stood out.

I work with large manufacturing plants in TN & Karnataka on machine safety — specifically
the gap between a certified safety system on paper and what's actually happening at
the man-machine interface on the shop floor.

Many plants I speak with are ISO 45001 certified but still see recurring near-misses
at [press lines / robotic cells / conveyor systems / packaging lines] — because the
standard doesn't prescribe specific guarding or interlock design.

I'd love to understand how [Company] is approaching this gap — whether you're already
ahead of it or if it's an active challenge.

15 minutes this week or next?

— [Your name]
Fortress Safety / VAYON Systems | [Phone]
```

## InMail C — Post-Incident Awareness / Safety Week

**Subject**: `One question — machine safety priorities at [Company]`

```
Hi [Name],

[Context: National Safety Week / World Safety Day / a recent industry incident in
their segment] has been top of mind this week.

I work with [Automotive / FMCG / Pharma] plants in TN & Karnataka on reducing
man-machine interface risks — practical interventions, not just compliance paperwork.

Three questions I ask every plant safety leader:
1. Where is your highest-frequency near-miss occurring?
2. Do your machine guarding standards match your current equipment age?
3. Has your risk assessment been updated since your last equipment change?

The answers usually reveal 2–3 high-impact, quick-win areas.

Would you be open to a 15-min call this week to explore what that looks like at
[Company]?

— [Your name]
```

- [ ] Pain point matches their industry segment (see CLAUDE.md Segment Reference)
- [ ] CTA is a specific, low-commitment ask (15 min call)
