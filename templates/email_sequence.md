# Email Sequence Templates — 3-Touch Sequence

> Spacing: Touch 1 on Day 1, Touch 2 on Day 5–6, Touch 3 on Day 10–12.
> Personalize [bracketed] fields for each account.
> Send from your professional domain email (not Gmail).

---

## TOUCH 1 — Industry Insight + Soft Ask

**Subject options** (A/B test):
- `[Segment] plants in TN — one safety gap that's easy to miss`
- `Quick question for [Company]'s safety team`
- `Machine safety in [Automotive/FMCG/Pharma] — one data point worth sharing`

```
Hi [Name],

[Personalisation line — 1 sentence referencing something specific:
e.g., "I came across [Company]'s ISO 45001 certification" or
"I noticed [Company] recently expanded your [Hosur / Coimbatore] facility."]

Here's something we see consistently across [segment] plants in South India:

Most companies have strong safety certifications and systems documented —
but 60–70% of near-misses still occur at the man-machine interface:
unguarded nip points, inadequate safety interlocks on older equipment,
or LOTO procedures that exist on paper but aren't followed on the floor.

It's rarely a culture problem. It's usually a gap in the physical safeguards.

I work with manufacturing plants in TN & Karnataka on exactly this — audits,
engineering controls, and safety integration to close that gap.

Would it make sense to spend 15 minutes comparing notes on how [Company]
approaches this? Happy to share what I've seen work well in similar plants.

Best,
[Your name]
[Title] | Fortress Safety / VAYON Systems
[Phone] | [Email]
```

## TOUCH 2 — Mini Case Study

**Subject options**:
- `What changed for a [segment] plant near [city] — 3-month outcome`
- `RE: [original subject line]` *(reply thread, no new subject)*
- `Quick follow-up — [Company] + machine safety`

```
Hi [Name],

Following up on my earlier message.

A quick example of what I mean in practice:

A [Tier-1 Automotive / large FMCG / mid-size Pharma] plant in [Chennai /
Coimbatore / Bengaluru] was ISO 45001 certified with a strong safety culture —
but was seeing 3–4 near-misses per month on their [press/conveyor/packaging]
lines.

We ran a structured machine safety audit, identified 12 guarding gaps and
3 interlock integrity issues, and helped them implement engineering controls
in a phased 8-week program.

Outcome: zero near-misses in the 3 months following implementation.
Their EHS head now has a clear forward roadmap instead of reactive firefighting.

That's the shift from compliance to genuine risk reduction.

Would a 15-minute call to explore whether there's a similar opportunity at
[Company] be worth it?

Best,
[Your name]
```
