### Case Study B — FMCG Packaging Line
```

recurring hand injuries on their high-speed packaging lines.

The issue wasn't awareness — it was fixed guarding that didn't account for
the cleaning-in-place (CIP) workflow. Operators were removing guards to clean
and forgetting to refit them.

Solution: guarding redesign for CIP access + sensor-based guard monitoring.
No injuries in 6 months following implementation.

Relevant for [Company]? Happy to share more. — [Your name]
```

## WhatsApp Guidelines

**DO:**
- Send between 9 AM – 6 PM on working days
- Keep messages under 150 words
- Always sign off with your name and company
- Offer something useful (checklist, insight, case study) — not just a sales ask

**DON'T:**
- Send PDFs or brochures unsolicited
- Send the same message to a contact twice in the same month
- Follow up on WhatsApp if the contact hasn't replied to 2 prior messages
- Use WhatsApp for a first introduction (LinkedIn or email first)
